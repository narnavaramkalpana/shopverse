Returns & Refunds

We want you to be completely satisfied with your purchase. If you need to return an item, we are here to help.

7-Day Return Window

You have 7 calendar days to return an item from the date you received it. To be eligible for a return, your item must be unused, in the same condition that you received it, and in the original packaging.

Refund Timeline

Once we receive your item at our warehouse, we will inspect it and notify you that we have received the returned item. We will immediately notify you on the status of your refund after inspecting the item. If approved, we will initiate a refund to your credit card or original method of payment, which clears in 3-5 business days.
